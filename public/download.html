Install

Get Orbit on your device

Your account works everywhere — sign in once on each device.

WindowsDesktop app with screen sharing and sound

Download Orbit.exe and open it. There's nothing to install, so you can keep it on your desktop or pin it to your taskbar.

Download for Windows

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. That message appears for new apps that haven't paid for a signing certificate yet.

LinuxA native app — AppImage runs on any distro

Grab the AppImage — one file, no install. Works on Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch, Mint, Debian and most other distros.

  1. Download Orbit.AppImage.
  2. Make it executable: right-click → Properties → Permissions → Allow executing, or run chmod +x Orbit.AppImage.
  3. Double-click it to launch, and sign in.
Download AppImage Debian / Ubuntu .deb

Prefer the .deb? Install it with sudo apt install ./Orbit.deb and Orbit shows up in your app menu.

iPhone & iPadAdd Orbit to your Home Screen from Safari

Open this page in Safari first. Other browsers on iPhone can't install apps.

  1. Tap the Share button On newer iPhones, tap ••• first, then Share.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open Orbit from your Home Screen and sign in.

It runs full screen with its own icon and always has the newest version. Calls stay connected while Orbit is open.

AndroidInstall Orbit from Chrome in one tap
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Install app On some phones it says Add to Home screen.
Meta QuestA floating Orbit window in your headset

Quickest: open the Browser in your headset, go to orbital.sbs and sign in.

As an app, right in the headset: with Developer Mode on, tap Download for Meta Quest below in the headset's Browser, then open Orbit-Quest.apk from your downloads and install it.

Or from a computer:

  1. Turn on Developer Mode for your headset in the Meta Horizon app on your phone. Menu → Devices → your headset → Headset settings → Developer Mode.
  2. On your computer, download Orbit-Quest.apk.
  3. Plug in your headset and install the file with SideQuest or Meta Quest Developer Hub.
  4. In the headset, open Library → Unknown Sources → Orbit.
Download for Meta Quest
XboxUse Orbit with your controller
  1. Open Microsoft Edge on your Xbox.
  2. Go to orbital.sbs and sign in.
  3. Orbit switches to controller mode on its own: move with the stick or D-pad, A to select, B to go back.

Tip: add it to your Edge favorites so it's one click next time.

Mac & any browserNothing to download

Orbit runs in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. Want it in your dock? In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar. In Safari on a Mac, choose File → Add to Dock.
