Features

Everything you need to hang out.

Crisp voice & video

Low-latency calls with noise suppression. Hop into a voice room and just talk.

Screen share with sound

Stream a game or your desktop in HD, audio included, so everyone's in on it.

Built for gamers

Live game status, an in-game overlay, and a talking ring so you see who's speaking.

Private by design

Two-step sign-in, blocking, invisible mode and read-receipt controls. No ads, ever.

Make it yours

Nine layouts, custom themes, animated profiles, banners, badges and custom emoji.

Everywhere you are

Windows, Linux, iPhone, Android, Quest and web — one account, synced across all.

Featherweight

Barely any memory or disk. Orbit stays fast on old laptops and low-end phones — not just the newest hardware.

Works anywhere

Light enough for slow connections, so it stays usable right across the world — wherever your friends are.
